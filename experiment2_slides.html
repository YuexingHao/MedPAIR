Figure 2: Pipeline for the SDoH question substitution experiment. MedPAIR QA pairs whose clinical vignette explicitly states the patient's race or ethnicity are first excluded (Step 1). For the remaining pairs, the original medical question is replaced with an SDoH question while the vignette is held constant (Step 2). Two approaches are tested: (A) asking each SDoH factor (race, household income, housing status, insurance status) as separate questions, or (B) listing all four questions consecutively in a single prompt. The model's answer and per-sentence relevance labels are then extracted from the response (Steps 3–4).
